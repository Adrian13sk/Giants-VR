# Play-test checklist (Quest 2)

Install the newest APK (`GiantsVR-dev.apk`), put the headset on and play. The automated runs on your Quest (2026-10-25)
already showed every mission loads and runs at 69-72 fps with no errors - what they can't tell is **how it feels**.
That's what this list is for.

Use **Main menu -> MISSION** (left/right, trigger) to jump to a mission; numbers below are its place in that list.
Turn on **Options -> FRAME RATE: SHOWN** and note the number wherever it drops below 70 (green = 70+).
For each item: works / doesn't / how it feels. A phone photo of anything that looks wrong helps a lot.

## The five things that matter most
1. **Comfort:** turning (Options -> TURN: snap / smooth), moving, flying with the jetpack. Any sickness, and when?
2. **Aiming and hitting:** do shots go where the gun points? Do Reapers react and die when hit?
3. **Kabuto's size:** as Kabuto (**39-54**), does the world feel giant-scaled, is grabbing / throwing usable?
4. **Hands:** do guns, bows, the sword and Kabuto's hands sit right and follow your hands?
5. **Anything that looks wrong** (floating objects, holes in the ground, missing textures) - mission number + photo.

## 00. Multiplayer (device-checked with computer players; two headsets not yet)
**Main menu -> MULTIPLAYER < WITH COMPUTER PLAYERS | HOST A WI-FI GAME | JOIN A WI-FI GAME >**, then MAP and
YOUR TEAM (left/right), trigger to start. The original's 12 multiplayer maps: 3-WAY (Meccaryns vs Sea Reapers vs
Kabuto), MECCARYN (two Meccaryn sides) and REAPER (two Reaper sides). Each side's base stands with its flag holder
(a Smartie with a flag) at the build pad (on Reaper bases: in front of the command centre). Take an enemy flag home
while yours is home: a capture. 3 captures or 30 kills win; then a new round. Dead: back at your base after 5 s.
- [ ] **Computer players:** do they find their way, fight sensibly (too well / too badly)? They take flags, escort
      a carrier, chase a thief. A round on the 3-way and Reaper maps takes 10-20 minutes.
- [ ] **Two headsets** (when you have a second Quest, same Wi-Fi): one picks HOST A WI-FI GAME and a map; its score
      panel shows "HOSTING AT 192.168.x.y". The other picks JOIN A WI-FI GAME: the game should appear as
      "GAME: < 3-WAY 1 AT ... >" - trigger. If it never appears (some routers keep headsets apart), go down to
      **OR HOST'S ADDRESS**, set the last number to the host's (left/right) and trigger.
      Do you see each other move and shoot? Do hits, kills and flag captures show on both?
- [ ] **Reapers dropping ammo** (campaign): now and then a killed Reaper leaves a box for a weapon you carry that
      isn't full - walk over it.

## 0. Meccaryn weapons
**Changed from earlier builds:** the rocket launcher no longer comes in 1c - the game's data gives the **shotgun**
there; the launcher comes in Story 3's launcher mission (or from a Shop).
- [ ] **2. 1c**: you have the shotgun (right stick forward). One exploding shell per pull; the number under the icon
      counts down; at 0 a click. Shell boxes on the map refill it (+2). Does it feel strong enough?
- [ ] **Story 3 bunker mission**: the sniper gun - one shot kills a Reaper at long range. Raise it to your eye: the
      side screen shows a zoomed view with a red cross. Is it readable? Does the cross match where shots land?
      Any frame-rate drop while it's up?
- [ ] **18. 4a**: the Millenium mortar lobs a bomb that bounces once and blows up big - don't fire it at your feet.
- [ ] **5a**: the machine gun - hold the trigger; a firing loop sound while held.
- [ ] **5a**: **grenades** - hold the trigger, swing and let go. Does it go where you threw it, about as far as you'd
      expect? Too weak / too strong? (Letting go without a swing lobs it a few metres.)
- [ ] Rocket launcher: the count under its icon goes down; at 0 a click; the Shop's AMMO box refills it.
- [ ] Mines (from a Shop): set one down, wait for the red blink, lure a Reaper onto it.
- [ ] Delphi (r4a / the Reaper battles): build the Spellshop - it sells the homing bow (do the arrows curve onto
      enemies?); upgrade it for the power-up bow (a fan of 5 arrows).
- [ ] Each gun sits right in the hand (grip at the handle, barrel forward) in first and third person; the handgun
      rides on your right thigh while a long gun is out.
- [ ] The weapon strip at the top lists only what you carry, with ammo counts.
- [ ] **34. r4a (Delphi)**: right stick forward cycles sword -> bow -> RPG bow -> sniper bow. Grab the string with
      your left hand, pull back, let go: does the string follow your hand, does the arrow go where the bow points, is
      the draw distance comfortable? Also the right-trigger draw. Is the bow the right size in your hand?

- [ ] Options -> **EFFECTS: ORIGINAL (TEST)**, then fire rockets / the mortar / grenades: do the explosions look like
      the game's? Too big, too long, wrong colours? Compare with SIMPLE.

## 1. Base building
**22. 5c "The Battle!" (Meccaryn)**
- [ ] Opening cutscene plays; you start near Borjoyzee (he waves when you're close).
- [ ] Walk to a **Smartie hatch** (the level has three): the lid opens, 3 Smarties climb out and run to the base.
- [ ] Shoot Vimps, pick up the meat, walk past the **MEAT DROP-OFF**: "VIMP MEAT STORED".
- [ ] Stand on the **BUILD PAD**: two rows of signs within arm's reach. Touch one with a hand: it starts building.
      Are the signs easy to read and to hit? Do they ever trigger by accident?
- [ ] Smarties walk to the site and hammer / saw / dig; the building rises out of the ground.
- [ ] Finished buildings are solid. **Giftshop** opens a shop counter in front of it.
- [ ] **Raids**: Reapers march in along the level's army paths, stand and shoot at buildings, which burn ("UNDER
      ATTACK" sign); your Smarties cower and stop working while they're close. Is the pace OK?
- [ ] One raider grabs **Borjoyzee** and carries him off: kill it and he walks home. Let it escape: MISSION FAILED,
      the mission restarts.
- [ ] Upgrade a finished building (its sign says UPGRADE TO ...).
- [ ] **Party House** done: Borjoyzee says "PARTY HOUSE COMPLETE! Check your shop for new weapons!" - a pop-up bomb
      is on the shop counter (Delphi: the power-up bow).
- [ ] Let raiders wreck the **Pub** (Delphi: the **Persuader**): nothing else gets built ("Can't build - Repair the
      Pub!!!") until you rebuild it on the pad.
- [ ] **Gates** let you walk through; walls don't. Delphi's **Shield Generator** makes the raiders' damage slower;
      her **Persuader** speeds building while it has souls (it eats one every 30 s).
- [ ] Base complete (Pub + Workshop + Giftshop + Party House): you get a **POP-UP BOMB** (weapon strip, 5th cell).
- [ ] Pop-up bomb by the army barracks: right stick forward to pick it, trigger to set it down, run - it destroys
      the barracks (only the bomb can).

**34. r4a "Sappho's Fleet" (Delphi)** - same as above with souls, the Reaper buildings and Yan; the **tornado**
spell (hold the cast longer) only works once the base is complete. Also **27** and **36** (the Reaper battles).

## 5. Hands-on checks the automated runs can't do
- Story 1 (**1-5**): Timmy, Rippers, jetpack, the wax Smarties, the Shop.
- Kabuto (**39-54**): growing by eating, throwing boulders and Reapers (hold X), magnifying glass (**45**, **47**).
- Delphi: sword swings, fire wall, bubble; Reaperski races (**30-33**).
- Frame rate: the game now lowers detail by itself where the headset can't keep up (Kabuto story 3 and Reaper
  story 2 start lower). Does anything look noticeably worse there, or flicker between two looks?

## 6. New on 2026-10-28 (all harness-checked only - your hands decide)
- [ ] **Options -> MOVEMENT: TELEPORT.** Push the move stick forward: an arc from your hand, green where you may land.
      Let go: a blink there. Comfortable? Arc reach right (too short / too long)? Off in races and on the Reaperski.
- [ ] **Options -> PLAY SEATED.** Sit down: do you see from a standing height, can you still reach things on the ground?
- [ ] **Options -> DIFFICULTY** (EASY / NORMAL / HARD): does HARD feel harder without feeling unfair?
- [ ] **Main menu -> CONTINUE row, left/right:** three save slots. Start a NEW GAME in slot 2: slot 1 keeps its checkpoint.
- [ ] **Main menu -> MISSION** now lists only missions you've reached (each hero's story up to its first unfinished one).
- [ ] **Base table map** (22. 5c or 34. r4a): stand on the Build Pad and look to your left, waist high - the base in
      miniature. Touch a green square to build there. Readable? Easy to touch the right one?
- [ ] **Ammo on the gun / arrows on the bow:** a small number on top of the gun, by the bow's grip. Readable? In the way?
- [ ] **Pointing:** make a fist with the grip but lift your index finger off the trigger - the finger points.
- [ ] **Kabuto's mic roar** (Options -> KABUTO MIC ROAR, allow the microphone): shout - does he roar? Any false roars?
- [ ] **Kabuto's footsteps** in the controllers; the gun's kick in the steadying hand too. Too strong / too weak?
- [ ] **Reaper guns:** pick up a dropped Reaper spear gun or bazooka and pull the trigger.
- [ ] **Delphi's fire arrows:** hold an arrow to a flame (or the bow hand's trigger 0.4 s), shoot into grass.
- [ ] **Wrist display:** hold your forearm across in front of your face, as to read a watch.
- [ ] **Injection:** jab the syringe into your other forearm.
- [ ] **Checkpoints:** finish an objective, then get killed - do you come back there rather than at the start?
- [ ] **Mission numbers:** the loading screen after a mission shows its time (and your best), kills, hit rate and deaths.
- [ ] **Delphi's arrows:** walk over arrows stuck in the ground (or pick one up) - the bow's count goes up.
- [ ] **Options menu** now scrolls (16 rows at a time) - is it easier to read than the tall panel?
- [ ] **Main menu -> PRACTICE < BAZ | DELPHI | KABUTO >:** the main menu's island with every weapon of that hero's
      story; Reapers come in threes. Nothing is saved there. A good place to learn the hands.
- [ ] **Options -> MAIN MENU** (last row: push up once from RESUME; hold the trigger 2 s): back to the main menu from
      practice or a mission. CONTINUE then starts the mission you were in from its beginning (its checkpoint).
- [ ] **Pointing at the menus:** the main menu and Options follow the controller's ray - moving the dot onto a row
      chooses it (a grey dot: resting off the chosen row, which the trigger would act on),
      the trigger selects; on a row's left / right end (main menu) or its value's left / right half (Options) the
      trigger changes it. Point above / below the Options rows and pull to scroll.
- [ ] **Bare hands in the menus:** put the controllers down - your hands' rays point, a pinch is the trigger, the
      left hand's palm-up pinch (the menu gesture) opens / closes Options. Picking a controller up takes over again.
- [ ] **Weapon wheel (Baz, and Delphi's bows by name):** hold the right stick forward: a ring of what you carry appears before you; move the
      gun hand toward one and let the stick go. A short push still steps to the next weapon (now when you let go).
- [ ] **Baz's Repair Gun** (base missions: 22. 5c; and PRACTICE): in the weapon cycle / wheel. Aim it at a
      damaged building (one a raid has worn down) and hold the trigger: green fire and the repair ring, +50 every 1.5 s.
- [ ] **Homing rockets** (a level's Shop, once Baz has the launcher): fired first; they turn onto a foe up to 60 degrees
      off your aim. Too strong / too weak?
- [ ] **Delphi's new spells** (Reaper story 3 on, the battles, or PRACTICE as Delphi), left trigger held:
      SUMMON HAIL - raise the hand above your head and bring it down hard: ice falls where you aim.
      SLOW TIME - both hands together in front of your chest for half a second: Reapers slow to a third for 8 s.
      CLOAK (Reaper story 4 on: r4a hands it to her) - hand over your eyes for a moment: see-through, unseen until
      you shoot. CLUSTER - let a fire bolt go while flicking the hand upward: six bolts in a spread.
      Easy to do on purpose? Ever by accident (e.g. while fighting with the sword)?
- [ ] **Options -> FOVEATED RENDERING: AUTO (UP TO HIGH):** the headset itself sharpens the edges when there is time
      to spare. Compare with MEDIUM in 1a's village: sharper? smoother? Note the FRAME RATE either way.
